# Requirements

Things we need from the school to go live.

| # | Item | What we need from you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Online fee payment | Open a Razorpay account for the school and share the keys with us |
| 2 | School email | An official school email (or SMTP details) so the system can send receipts and notices |
| 3 | WhatsApp | A WhatsApp Business number and login so we can send fee receipts and reminders |
| 4 | Login OTP | Tell us: send codes by **email** or by **SMS**? (For SMS, school must register with an SMS provider) |
| 5 | Biometric / RFID | Machine make, model, and how to export punch data |
| 6 | Optional extras | Tell us if you also want Paytm, bus GPS, or SMS alerts |

Share passwords and keys only with our team contact, not in a group chat.
